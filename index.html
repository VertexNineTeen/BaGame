Dalcoat

You and your Partner (top) play against Left and Right.

  • Everyone picks a card from the deck. The lowest card deals.
  • The player next to the dealer gets 5 cards first and secretly hides one. Its suit is the trump.
  • Capture tricks that contain the 10s.
  • Follow the suit that was led if you can. Nobody stops you, but if you're caught breaking it, your team loses the round.
  • Can't follow suit? Tap Show trump to reveal it.
  • Highest trump wins, otherwise highest card of the led suit.
  • 3 or 4 tens wins the round. At 2–2, the team with 7+ tricks wins.
